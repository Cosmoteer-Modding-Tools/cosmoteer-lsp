## ApplyToAllHitPartTiles
`bool` · optional

Whether the effect should apply to each tile of the hit part, or just the tile matching the hit location. Only applicable to tile-layer statuses.

## DivideValueByPartArea
`bool` · optional

Whether the status value will be divided by the tile-area of the hit part.

## DivideValueByPartHealth
`bool` · optional

Whether the status value will be divided by the max health of the hit part.

## DivideValueByPartHealthFactor
`bool` · optional

Whether the status value will be divided by the part's current health percentage.
