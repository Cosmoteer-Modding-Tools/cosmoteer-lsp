## CoverageHitTolerance
`float` · required

Distance, in screen pixels, within which the mouse grabs a coverage handle when the editor has full interaction.

## CoveragePartObstructedHitTolerance
`float` · required

Grab distance, in screen pixels, used instead of [[Cosmoteer.Game.AdjustWeaponCoverageGuiRules.CoverageHitTolerance]] when the mouse is over a part and the editor is in its reduced interaction mode.

## CoverageMinimumRange
`float` · required

The shortest custom range, in tiles, a player can set. Dragged ranges are clamped to it and shorter custom ranges are refused.

## CoverageInfiniteRangeFallback
`float` · required

Range, in tiles, the editor draws and edits for a weapon whose range is infinite.

## CoverageRangeSnapIncrement
`float` · required

Step, in tiles, that dragged ranges snap to in normal precision.

## CoverageFineRangeSnapIncrement
`float` · required

Step, in tiles, that dragged ranges snap to in fine precision. Half of it is also the tolerance for treating two ranges as equal.

## CoverageAngleSnapIncrement
`float` · required

Step, in degrees, that dragged arc edges snap to in normal precision.

## CoverageFineAngleSnapIncrement
`float` · required

Step, in degrees, that dragged arc edges snap to in fine precision. Half of it is also the tolerance for treating two arcs as equal and for treating an arc's two edges as converged into one line.

## CoverageIcon
`Sprite` · required

Icon of the weapon coverage editor button in the build screen and on the parts card.

## CoverageCursor
`Cursor` · required

Mouse cursor shown while a coverage handle is hovered or dragged.

## CoverageResetSound
`ISoundEffect` · required

Sound played when weapon coverages are reset to their defaults.

## CoverageThicknessScale
`float` · required

Thickness multiplier for coverage lines and rims, applied after the zoom scaling from [[Cosmoteer.Game.AdjustWeaponCoverageGuiRules.CoverageThicknessScaleExponent]].

## CoverageHandleThickness
`float` · required

Thickness multiplier used instead of [[Cosmoteer.Game.AdjustWeaponCoverageGuiRules.CoverageThicknessScale]] for the hovered or dragged handle.

## CoveragePhysicalLimitThicknessScale
`float` · required

Thickness multiplier for the dashed lines and rim that show a weapon's physical range and arc limits.

## CoverageThicknessScaleExponent
`float` · required

Exponent applied to the camera's world scale before it multiplies every coverage line and rim thickness. 0 keeps a constant world width and 1 scales fully with zoom.

## CoverageColor
`Color` · required

Color of coverage lines and rims, also used for the floating text shown on reset.

## CoverageHandleColor
`Color` · required

Color of the hovered or dragged handle.

## CoverageMirroredColor
`Color` · required

Color used instead of [[Cosmoteer.Game.AdjustWeaponCoverageGuiRules.CoverageColor]] for weapons that are edited as a symmetry mirror of the dragged one.

## CoveragePhysicalLimitColor
`Color` · required

Color of the dashed lines and rim that show a weapon's physical limits.

## CoverageRim
`CircleRenderer` · required

Renderer for the curved outer edge of a coverage sector at its range.

## CoverageHandleRim
`CircleRenderer` · required

Renderer used instead of [[Cosmoteer.Game.AdjustWeaponCoverageGuiRules.CoverageRim]] when the range edge is the hovered or dragged handle.

## CoverageLine
`CappedLine` · required

Line drawn along the straight sides of each coverage sector, and along a single direction when the arc has converged to zero width.

## CoverageRestLine
`CappedLine` · required

Dotted line drawn from a turret toward the direction it rests at when it has no target, the handle a player drags to set [[Cosmoteer.Ships.Parts.Weapons.WeaponCoverage.RestPosition]]. Not drawn while the arc has converged to zero width.

## CoverageRestLineLength
`float` · required

Length, in tiles, of [[Cosmoteer.Game.AdjustWeaponCoverageGuiRules.CoverageRestLine]]. Vanilla uses `10`.

## CoverageRestLineDotLengthPerThickness
`float` · required

Target dot length of [[Cosmoteer.Game.AdjustWeaponCoverageGuiRules.CoverageRestLine]], as a multiple of its thickness. The dot count snaps to a power of two like [[Cosmoteer.Game.AdjustWeaponCoverageGuiRules.CoveragePhysicalLimitDashLengthPerThickness]]. Vanilla uses `1` because its line texture is square.

## CoverageArcFill
`CircleRenderer` · required

Renderer for the translucent fill over each turret's coverage sector. Its thickness is rescaled to fill the sector out to its range.

## CoverageArcFillMaxOverlap
`int` · required

Maximum number of arc fills drawn over each other at any point, enforced with the stencil buffer. Values above 255 do not work.

## CoverageUnaffectedAlphaMultiplier
`float` · required

Opacity multiplier for the coverage lines of weapons the hovered or dragged handle will not change.

## CoveragePhysicalLimitRim
`CircleRenderer` · required

Renderer for the dashed rim at a turret's physical range. Drawn only when that range is finite.

## CoveragePhysicalLimitLine
`CappedLine` · required

Line drawn dashed along the sides of a weapon's physical arc, or along its direction for a fixed weapon.

## CoveragePhysicalLimitDashLengthPerThickness
`float` · required

Target dash length of the physical limit rim and lines, as a multiple of their thickness. The dash count snaps to a power of two so dashes are inserted between existing ones as the length grows.
