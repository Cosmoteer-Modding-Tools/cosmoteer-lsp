# Cosmoteer.Simulation.MediaEffects.MediaEffectRules

One piece of the sound and visuals an event produces, such as a sprite, a beam, a particle system or a sound. A modder writes one of its concrete types.

> A group in this slot picks its class with `Type = …`:
>
> - [`ArcShield`](./Cosmoteer.Simulation.MediaEffects.ArcShieldEffectRules.md): Draws the curved shield wall a shield generator projects, fading in and out and matching the generator's radius and arc.
> - [`Audio`](./Cosmoteer.Simulation.MediaEffects.AudioEffectRules.md): Plays a sound at the effect's position, with volume and pitch that follow camera distance, zoom and the effect's intensity.
> - [`Beam`](./Cosmoteer.Simulation.MediaEffects.BeamEffectRules.md): Draws a straight textured beam out from the source, stretched to the length the firing weapon reports.
> - [`PartQuad`](./Cosmoteer.Simulation.MediaEffects.PartQuadEffectRules.md): Draws a sprite stretched across a whole part, for glows and overlays that ride along as the part moves.
> - [`Particles`](./Cosmoteer.Simulation.MediaEffects.ParticleEffectRules.md): Plays a particle system at the effect's position, pairing a shared `Def` that describes the particles with an `EmitterDef` that sets how fast they come out.
> - [`Quad`](./Cosmoteer.Simulation.MediaEffects.QuadEffectRules.md): Draws a single sprite at the effect's position, for muzzle flashes, glows and impact flares.
> - [`Random`](./Cosmoteer.Simulation.MediaEffects.RandomMediaEffectRules.md): Picks one of several media effect sets at random every time the effect plays, weighted by each option's `RandomWeight`.
> - [`ScreenShake`](./Cosmoteer.Simulation.MediaEffects.ScreenShakeEffectRules.md): Shakes the camera, harder the closer the view is to the effect and fading out over `Duration`.
> - [`TileQuad`](./Cosmoteer.Simulation.MediaEffects.TileQuadEffectRules.md): Draws a sprite over a single ship tile, for per-tile overlays such as fire, foam and scorch marks.

## Delay
`number (seconds)` · optional

Seconds the effect waits after being triggered before it actually plays. Applies only to one-shot playback, continuous playback always starts immediately. Being modifiable, it can be driven by buffs carried in the effect's parameters.

## IgnoreIntensity
`bool` · optional

Has no effect. The game stores the value but no media effect type ever reads it.
